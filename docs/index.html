Features

Everything you need to read, nothing you don't

Built for reading first — fast to open, pleasant to scroll, out of your way.

Full GFM rendering

Headings, tables, task lists, fenced code blocks, blockquotes, images and videos — rendered exactly as intended.

Syntax highlighting

JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Rust, Go, Bash, SQL, CSS, HTML/XML and JSON, out of the box.

Dark & light themes

Auto-detects your system preference and lets you toggle anytime from the toolbar.

Open any way you like

Ctrl+O, drag & drop onto the window, file association (double-click) or right-click → "Open with MD Viewer".

Relative paths that work

Images and links relative to the .md file resolve automatically — screenshots and assets just show up.

Zoom & pan, remembered

Ctrl+wheel from 80% to 200%, Ctrl+drag to pan. Your zoom level is kept between sessions.

Ghost-light & fast

A ~2 MB installer on Windows. Opens instantly, sits in the tray of your mind — barely there.

Truly native

Built on Tauri 2: WebView2 on Windows, native WebKit on macOS and Linux. No Electron bloat.
